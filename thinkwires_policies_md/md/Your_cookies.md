What is a cookie?

Cookies are files sent to and stored on your computer when you visit certain web pages.

These files make it possible to remember your preferences, facilitate your navigation and thus improve your experience as a user.

Some cookies require your consent, which you may withdraw at any time. Disabling certain cookies could have the effect of reducing accessibility to all or part of the Services offered by the THINKWIRES website.

How to manage cookies?

When you log in for the first time, the cookie banner allows you to choose your cookie preferences.

Several choices are available to you:

- You can accept the deposit of cookies using the “Accept” box.

- You can refuse the deposit of cookies using the “Refuse all” box.

- You can set your preferences at any time, using the “Customize” box.

The cookie manager is available at the bottom of every page of the THINKWIRES website. This allows you to change your choices regarding the deposit of different types of cookies at any time.

Trackers whose purpose is limited to the operation and management measures of the website, are not subject to consent.

As such, you can also configure your browser to delete the cookies deposited during your browsing or to limit their deposit globally.
