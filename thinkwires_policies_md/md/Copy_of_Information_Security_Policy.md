# PURPOSE AND SCOPE

This policy establishes the framework for protecting and managing Thinkwires information systems as vital organizational assets. Its purpose is to ensure the confidentiality, integrity, availability, and resilience of information systems across the organization. The policy applies to all personnel and third parties who access, support, develop, or manage Thinkwires information systems.

The objectives of this policy are to:

- Maintain secure, reliable, and uninterrupted information system operations;

- Preserve the integrity and validity of organizational data;

- Enable timely and effective recovery from system disruptions or incidents; and

- Protect Thinkwires information, software, and hardware assets from unauthorized access, loss, or damage.

Information assets, software assets, and hardware assets—whether hosted internally or externally—collectively constitute the Thinkwires information systems. The scope of this policy includes personally owned devices when they are used to access Thinkwires data or systems.
