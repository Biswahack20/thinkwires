## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an elite, high-trust digital environment tailored for global enterprise talent acquisition leaders, CTOs, and high-impact engineering candidates. The brand tone balances executive authority with modern software engineering agility: institutional confidence, velocity, and technical excellence.

The aesthetic fuses **Corporate Modern** with **Elevated Glassmorphic Accents**:

- Deep, prestige architectural foundations anchored by midnight slate and deep maritime blue.
- Precision cobalt blue signaling intelligence, stability, and digital clarity.
- Energetic tech emerald green accents celebrating global network velocity, successful placements, and live talent status.
- Crisp white content surfaces floating over hyper-subtle radial blue glows, micro-dot matrix patterns, and hairline gradient borders (1px border treatments with translucent highlights).
- Clean interactive state transitions featuring micro-elevation, directional glow lines, and executive-tier typographic hierarchy that eliminates visual noise and builds instant enterprise credibility.

## Colors

The color palette translates enterprise scale and high-level engineering precision into functional chromatic tiers:

- **Primary (`#1D4ED8`) & Vibrant Tech Cobalt (`#2563EB`)**: Drives primary calls-to-action, active selection rings, verified talent badges, and critical navigation items.
- **Secondary (`#0A2540`) & Executive Navy (`#0F172A`)**: Forms the structural base—used for primary text, deep hero footers, enterprise trust bars, and high-contrast statistical callouts.
- **Tertiary & Emerald Network (`#10B981`, `#22C55E`)**: Anchors live availability indicators, success metrics (e.g., "98% placement accuracy", "Interview within 48 hrs"), and positive status pips.
- **Surface & Backgrounds**: Default canvas uses clean, bright white (`#FFFFFF`) layered over neutral slate tints (`#F8FAFC`, `#F1F5F9`). Dark contrast zones leverage `#0A2540` layered over `#0F172A` with ambient cobalt gradient wash overlays.

## Typography

The typography leverages **Plus Jakarta Sans** uniformly across display, body, and micro-labels to achieve contemporary geometric precision while preserving high technical readability.

- **Display & Headlines**: Tightly tracked (`-0.01em` to `-0.02em`) with strong weights (`700` and `800`) to communicate institutional strength and clarity. Hero headings feature gradient text masks blending bright cobalt into deep navy on light backgrounds, or white into ice blue on dark hero bands.
- **Body Content**: Set at `400` and `500` weights with generous proportional line heights (`150%` to `155%`) to maximize legibility when presenting technical role requisitions, scope statements, and compliance documentation.
- **Labels & Micro-Tags**: Elevated with `600` weight and positive letter-spacing (`0.01em` - `0.02em`) for crisp readability on pill tags, salary badges, and skill taxonomy chips.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is anchored on an 8pt architectural rhythm structured within a 12-column responsive fluid grid:

- **Desktop (>= 1280px)**: 12 columns, `gutter-desktop: 2rem`, maximum content width of `1240px`, and outer margin padding of `5rem` to create generous negative space typical of high-end B2B platforms.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px)**: 8 columns, `gutter: 1.5rem`, outer margin `2rem`. Two-column service grids and modular search filters reflow into collapsible drawers.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns, `gutter: 1rem`, outer margin `1.25rem`. Job cards and client logos stack cleanly, hero CTAs stretch full-width for effortless tap targets.
- **Vertical Rhythm**: Section pacing alternates between dense information blocks (`space-xl`) and breathing transition bands (`space-2xl`) to avoid visual fatigue during complex candidate reviews.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a refined layered approach combining crisp 1px borders, ambient color shadows, and subtle glassmorphic tiers:

- **Flat/Base Surface**: Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) or cool slate (`#F8FAFC`) with a crisp `1px solid #E2E8F0` border.
- **Card Default (Elevation 1)**: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)` paired with a soft top hairline highlight (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)`).
- **Card Interactive / Hover (Elevation 2)**: `0 12px 24px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.06)`, with border color transitioning to `rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.4)`. The component subtly translates `-2px` on the Y-axis.
- **Glassmorphic Filter Bars & Sticky Headers**: `background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85); backdrop-filter: blur(12px); border-bottom: 1px solid rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8)`. On dark modules, this switches to `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.8)` with border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`.
- **Modals & Flyouts (Elevation 3)**: `0 25px 50px -12px rgba(10, 37, 64, 0.25)` complemented by a deep navy translucent backdrop (`rgba(10, 37, 64, 0.6)`) with `4px` blur.

## Shapes

The geometric framework balances executive rigor with approachable modernity:

- **Container Surfaces (Cards, Panels, Modals)**: Use standard roundedness (`0.5rem` / `8px` for inputs and secondary containers; `1rem` / `16px` for primary job cards and service feature modules).
- **Interactive Buttons**: Styled with `0.5rem` (`8px`) or full pill (`9999px`) options. High-visibility hero conversion actions leverage the pill contour to draw immediate focus.
- **Status Badges & Category Chips**: Strict pill geometry (`rounded-full` / `9999px`), giving them a polished token-like appearance that cleanly differentiates them from rectangular action buttons and data cards.
- **Avatar & Icon Badges**: `0.75rem` (`12px`) soft squircle shape for technological brand mark containers, avoiding raw circles to maintain an enterprise software feeling.

## Components

### Buttons

- **Primary CTA**: Cobalt Blue (`#1D4ED8`) background, white label, subtle top inner glow (`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`), `rounded-lg` or pill silhouette. Hover: `#2563EB` with `0 6px 16px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.3)`.
- **Secondary / Outline**: Transparent fill, `1.5px` border in `#0A2540` or `#E2E8F0`, deep slate text (`#0F172A`). Hover: `#F8FAFC` background with border `#CBD5E1`.
- **Ghost Action**: Text only with trailing chevron; blue hover text with smooth `2px` horizontal icon shift.

### Job & Talent Cards

- Constructed with a white surface, `1px solid #E2E8F0`, and `1rem` corner radius.
- Header row features company logo squircle, job title, and a prominent live status pill (e.g., "Immediate Hire" in emerald tint `#ECFDF5` with `#065F46` text and an animated green pulsing dot).
- Mid-section includes a structured horizontal metadata list (Location, Experience, Salary / Rate, Contract Type) paired with light neutral icons.
- Skill tags row at the bottom displaying matching technologies (e.g., "SAP ABAP", "Kubernetes", "Next.js") followed by an "Apply Now" button and quick "View Details" text link.

### Chips & Badges

- **Status Pills**: Height `26px`, padding `0 10px`, typography `label-sm`, rounded `9999px`.
  - Active/Verified: Light emerald background (`#ECFDF5`), emerald border (`#A7F3D0`), dark emerald text (`#047857`).
  - Tech Category: Cool slate background (`#F1F5F9`), slate border (`#E2E8F0`), deep navy text (`#334155`).
  - High Priority: Light cobalt background (`#EFF6FF`), cobalt border (`#BFDBFE`), bright cobalt text (`#1D4ED8`).

### Form Inputs & Search Module

- **Interactive Talent Search Bar**: Floating unified container with `12px` border radius, elevated shadow, hosting search keyword, location selector, and experience filter separated by vertical `1px` dividers, terminating in an integrated primary search button.
- **Field Inputs**: Height `44px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, text `#0F172A`, placeholder `#94A3B8`. Focused state: border `#2563EB` with `0 0 0 3px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.15)`.

### Metrics & Stat Callouts

- Large numbers set in `stat-counter` typography using deep navy or dynamic gradient, topped or paired with a micro emerald indicator (e.g., "+", "%").
- Accompanying label set in `label-sm` uppercase with neutral slate tone (`#64748B`).

### Checkboxes & Radios

- Size `18px`, `4px` border radius for checkboxes, full circle for radios.
- Unchecked: `1.5px solid #CBD5E1` on white background.
- Checked: `#1D4ED8` solid fill with white checkmark icon and slight scale spring animation.
